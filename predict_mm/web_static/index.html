Depth protection thresholds

Q is your order size. D is the total external bid size strictly above your buy price, excluding your own orders. Cancellation and recovery thresholds use the greater of the minimum shares and the multiplier × Q. Yes and No are checked separately, including orders at the minimum tick.

A rapid decline must meet both the percentage threshold and the low remaining-depth condition. The lookback is not the polling interval. Resuming requires cooldown and stable depth, with another check before submission. This may reduce quoting and cannot guarantee that orders will not fill.
